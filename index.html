FAQ

Do you sell or reserve Pokémon cards?

No. MSRP Squad sends alerts only. We don't sell, hold, reserve or ship any product, and we never check out for you. You buy directly from the retailer.

Will I definitely get the product?

No one can promise that. Popular drops can sell out in minutes. Alerts give you a head start, not a guarantee. Always confirm price and stock on the retailer's site.

Which stores do you cover?

Right now, Walmart.com: we check Walmart's official product API about once a minute and only alert when Walmart itself is the seller. Best Buy alerts are planned for Squad Pro once Best Buy's API approves our key. Target, Amazon and Pokémon Center aren't covered yet. We'll announce each new store in Discord when it actually goes live.

Do you cover Walmart drawings?

Walmart now sells some of the hottest boxes through timed drawings instead of a normal restock. Our engine doesn't detect those automatically. When Walmart announces a drawing, we post the window in Discord so you can enter yourself; entries and picks are entirely Walmart's.

Are free alerts delayed?

No. Free alerts post in #free-alerts the moment we detect a restock or preorder, the same moment Squad gets them. Free alerts are the basic version: product, price vs MSRP, IN STOCK or PREORDER, and the link.

So what does Squad add?

The “should I buy it” layer: a dated resale reference, the spread and our “worth it?” verdict, plus the retailer limit, on every alert. Pings only for the sets and product types you pick (or All Alerts for everything), alerts when Walmart's own price drops below MSRP, reminders 30 minutes before Walmart drawings and Pokémon Center queues, a weekly “Buy or Skip” report every Sunday, and the sightings and price-check forums.

What does “worth it?” mean?

On Squad alerts, we compare the retail price to a recent resale reference, either the TCGplayer market price or recent eBay sold listings, and show which one and its date. That helps you decide quickly whether a product is worth chasing. It's general information, not financial or investment advice.

Founding Squad and Squad Monthly include a 3-day free trial. If you cancel in Whop before it ends, you aren't charged. After that it's $5.99/month (Founding) or $7.99/month (regular) until you cancel.

Yes. Alerts are delivered in our Discord server. Whop connects your Discord account and assigns your role when you join.
