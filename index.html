A few things to know.

Will an alert guarantee I get the product?

No. Stock and prices can change before checkout. Always verify the retailer's page. We send alerts; we do not reserve products, buy for you or access your retailer account.

What does Squad add?

More control over supported set and product-type pings, dated market references when available, Walmart price-drop alerts, and the Squad discussion channels. Target's local beta remains in the free feed.

Are free alerts deliberately delayed?

No. The free feed posts when the worker detects an eligible change. Retailer data, delivery and availability can change; there is no guaranteed alert speed.

How do I get into Discord after joining?

Open MSRP Squad in Whop, choose its Discord app, connect the Discord account you want to use, then select Claim Access. If access looks wrong, contact #support or support@msrpsquad.com.

How does Founding Squad work?

The first 100 members can join for $5.99/month, locked while they remain subscribed. The offer ends Nov 6, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT or when those spots are taken. If you cancel and return later, regular pricing applies.

What about cancellations and refunds?

Cancel in Whop before your monthly trial ends to avoid the first charge. Forgot to cancel? Email within 48 hours of the first charge for a full refund. Annual plans are refundable within 14 days. See the full refund policy.
