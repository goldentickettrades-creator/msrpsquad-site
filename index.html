FAQ

Do you sell or reserve Pokémon cards?

No. MSRP Squad sends alerts only. We don't sell, hold, reserve or ship any product, and we never check out for you. You buy directly from the retailer.

Will I definitely get the product?

No one can promise that. Popular drops can sell out in minutes. Alerts give you a head start, not a guarantee. Always confirm price and stock on the retailer's site.

Which stores do you cover?

Right now, Walmart.com: we check Walmart's official product API about once a minute and only alert when Walmart itself is the seller. Best Buy is coming as soon as its API approves our key. Target, Amazon and Pokémon Center aren't covered yet. We'll announce each new store in Discord when it actually goes live.

Do you cover Walmart drawings?

Walmart now sells some of the hottest boxes through timed drawings instead of a normal restock. Our engine doesn't detect those automatically. When Walmart announces a drawing, we post the window in Discord so you can enter yourself; entries and picks are entirely Walmart's.

Why are free alerts delayed?

Squad members fund the service, so they get alerts first. Free members still see every alert about 15 minutes later, which is often still in time for slower restocks.

What does “worth it?” mean?

We compare the retail price to a recent resale reference so you can decide quickly whether a product is worth chasing. It's general information, not financial or investment advice.

How does the free trial work?

Squad Monthly includes a 3-day free trial. If you cancel in Whop before it ends, you aren't charged. After that it's $7.99/month until you cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Forgot to cancel your trial? Email us within 48 hours of the first charge for a full refund. Annual plans are refundable within 14 days. Full details in our refund policy.

Do I need Discord?

Yes. Alerts are delivered in our Discord server. Whop connects your Discord account and assigns your role when you join.

How do I get help?

Email support@msrpsquad.com or open a ticket in the Discord.
