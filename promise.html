The MSRP Promise

What every MSRP Squad alert has to pass before it reaches your Discord, and what we will never do.

1. How we check each store

  • Walmart: our own monitor watches products sold by Walmart itself, plus a licensed restock feed.
  • Target, Pokémon Center and other retailers: a licensed restock feed that our system checks every 15 seconds.
  • Free gets Walmart and Target alerts 2 minutes after Squad. Squad gets Walmart, Target and Pokémon Center as soon as we see them.
  • Coverage is not every product or every drop, and no alert speed is guaranteed. If something breaks, we say so in Discord.

2. The 15% price cap

An item has to be at MSRP or no more than 15% above it, or it doesn't get posted. If the price is missing or unreadable, it isn't posted either.

3. Each store's own MSRP

Stores don't all charge the same list price for the same product, so we check each one against that store's own price, with the source and date on file. If a store's price is more than 25% above standard list price, we don't treat it as MSRP and use standard list price instead. Every alert shows the price and the MSRP side by side.

4. Marketplace filter

We only post listings sold by the retailer itself. Third-party marketplace sellers and links that leave the retailer's site are filtered out.

5. One alert per drop

Each product, store and alert type gets one alert, then a 15-minute cooldown. No repeat pings for the same restock.

6. Alerts only, no bots

We send alerts. We never buy for you, log in to your retailer accounts, join queues for you or run auto-checkout. Walmart alerts include an add-to-cart button for Squad that only puts the item in your own cart; you still check out yourself. Queue and drawing posts are labeled as retailer events, not stock.

7. Try it free, cancel anytime

Squad monthly plans start with a 5-day free trial. Cancel in Whop before it ends and you're not charged. After that you can cancel anytime in Whop with no fees. Forgot to cancel? Email us within 48 hours of the first charge for a full refund. See the refund policy.
